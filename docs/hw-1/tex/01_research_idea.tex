\section{Research Idea}
\label{sec:research_idea}

\subsection{Research Idea}

This research paper investigates whether multilingual Large Language Models (LLMs) give more credibility to evidence based solely on its language when resolving direct factual contradictions in Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

\subsection{Research Question}

When a multilingual LLM is presented with directly conflicting factual claims in English and Korean, does it systematically favor the claim written in English regardless of the query language and its prior knowledge?
