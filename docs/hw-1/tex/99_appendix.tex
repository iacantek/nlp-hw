\subsection{AI Usage}

AI-assisted tools were used as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Gemini\footnote{\url{https://gemini.google.com}}:} Literature discovery and identification of related work.
    \item \textbf{NotebookLM\footnote{\url{https://notebooklm.google.com}}:} Document parsing and information extraction from surveyed papers.
    \item \textbf{Antigravity\footnote{\url{https://antigravity.google}} (Gemini 3.8 Flash):} Editorial assistance for stylistic refinement and structural improvements.
\end{itemize}

All retrieved references, citations, and extracted facts were manually verified by the author. The research design, critical analysis, and drafting of all text represent the author's original work.
